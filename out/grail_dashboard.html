Who vaulted their own cards?

Every physical card entering the vault mints tokens (the reserve's tokens-per-card, usually 10,000) straight to the submitter's wallet. If the recipient is Grail's inventory contract, team wallet or another Grail address, Grail vaulted the card itself; anyone else is a user vaulting a card.

Physical redemptions (burns)

Burning a full card's worth of tokens mints a claim NFT to the redeemer; sending it to Grail submits the claim. Grail burns the claim when the card ships, or burns it and re-mints the tokens if the redemption is cancelled. Each row traces where the tokens came from and how the claim ended.

Snipers

A snipe is a first buy within 5 minutes (150 blocks) of a pool's first liquidity. Sniper = 2+ tokens or a same-block buy; serial sniper = 3+ tokens or 2+ same-block buys.
